---
name: lens-wear-validation
description: Validate Lens core functions on Wear OS without overstating emulator evidence.
---
Only manually started/stopped microphone capture; visible recording indicator and OS permissions.
Emulator fixture WAV tests must be labeled fixture tests. Record ABI, OS, package/install bytes,
full process-tree RAM, startup, latency, model unload/reload and offline storage/recovery.
Emulator success does not establish physical-watch microphone, thermals or battery performance.
Standalone local watch functionality is unverified until the actual device workload is measured.
