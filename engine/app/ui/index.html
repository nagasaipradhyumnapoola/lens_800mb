Privacy and capture

Screen observation

When on, Lens reads text from the window you are using (accessibility text first, on-device OCR only when needed) and keeps it encrypted on this PC. Nothing is uploaded.

Microphone

When on, Lens listens to your default microphone and turns speech into text on this PC. It keeps the text, not the recording.

Phone link

Lets a phone or watch you pair send notes and requests to this PC over your local network.

Pause

Stops all capture immediately without changing your choices above. Also in the tray icon.

Start with Windows

Starts the Lens background agent and its tray icon when you sign in.

Your memory, kept on this PC

Lens remembers what you see and say, so you can ask about it later. It captures only what you switch on in the next step. Until then nothing is captured.

  • Screen: text from the window you are using, read through Windows accessibility, with on-device text recognition only when needed.
  • Microphone: speech, turned into text on this PC. The recording is not kept.
  • Phone link: notes and requests from a phone or watch you pair, over your local network.

Memories are stored encrypted in , readable only by your Windows account. Nothing is uploaded. You can pause, change these choices or delete memories at any time.

Your choices

Each one is off until you turn it on. You can change them later under Privacy.
